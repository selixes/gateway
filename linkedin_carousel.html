The 3-Layer Gateway Control Plane

You cannot solve infrastructure instability with prompt engineering. Reliability belongs in the transport layer.

1
Trajectory Instability Guards:
Maintains a rolling sliding-window SHA-256 hash of (tool_name + normalized_args). If 3 consecutive calls repeat the trajectory under error, an instant Cognitive Intercept aborts the loop.
2
Microsecond Atomic Session Budgets:
Redis INCRBYFLOAT evaluates spend before every outbound token leaves the perimeter. If a session breaches its cap (e.g. $0.15), it terminates at the edge.
3
Sub-16ms Multi-Cloud Circuit Breakers:
Instantly hot-swaps failing upstream providers (OpenAI ➔ Anthropic ➔ Gemini ➔ local Ollama Llama-3) in ~16ms without dropping client WebSockets.
